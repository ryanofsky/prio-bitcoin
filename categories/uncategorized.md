## Covers

Every assessed PR that the model placed in none of the categories listed
in `not_in` above. The list is computed by the site, not judged by a
model, and exists to catch PRs falling through the cracks: a PR here
means either a category definition has a gap in its "Covers" text, the
pre-filter hints (labels, paths, keywords) never put the PR in front of
the right category, or the PR fits nowhere and that is fine.

Only the categories named above count. A temporary or one-off category
can exist alongside them without shrinking this list, so when a new
permanent category is added, add its name to `not_in` here.

## What matters here

Nothing is ranked. Rows are newest first. The Judged column says which
categories the model was asked about and the reason each gave, which is
the starting point for fixing a definition.
